## Tokens

- Signature brand color: `#971CE6` (Cryptita Violet)
- Deep ink: `#17131D`
- Warm paper: `#F8F5F0`
- Soft lilac: `#EDE4F7`
- Muted copy: `#6B6473`
- Border: `rgba(23, 19, 29, 0.12)`
- Heading/body font: Poppins
- Motion: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)

## Asset rules

Use the deployment URLs exactly as listed. Do not reference the local files from runtime code. Keep text off hero artwork and maintain readable contrast by placing copy on solid or controlled overlay surfaces.
